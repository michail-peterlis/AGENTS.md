# Policy: Evidence and Provenance

Important derived results should be traceable to their origin.

Preserve where applicable:

- source identifier;
- source revision/version;
- source hash;
- record identity;
- configuration;
- producer version;
- transformation version;
- evidence population;
- lineage;
- benchmark/test command;
- output/status.

Distinguish:

- asserted;
- observed;
- inferred;
- predicted;
- reviewed;
- unknown;
- conflicting;
- absent.

Do not collapse records merely because their normalized projection is equal when
their source evidence differs.

Never rewrite failed or historical evidence to make the current state cleaner.
Supersede it explicitly.
